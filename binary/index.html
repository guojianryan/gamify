Decimal keypad

How a row works

  1. Number on the right. Click bits until the 1s add up to it. The row clears on its own.
  2. Blank on the right. The bits are fixed. Type their total and press Enter.
  3. The column fills. Six rows fit. One more arrival ends the round.

Click a row to select it. On a bit row, keys 1–8 flip columns from the left. On a decimal row, the number keys type. Enter submits. P pauses.

Ways to see a byte

  • The rightmost bit is the 1. It is on for every odd number, and off for every even number.
  • Take the largest place value that still fits, subtract it, and repeat with what is left.
  • One less than a place value is a run of 1s to its right. 16 − 1 is 0000 1111. 128 − 1 is 0111 1111.
  • All eight bits on is 255. An IPv4 octet lives in that range.

Eight clears raise the level. The rows then come faster, and the numbers get larger. Scores stay in this browser.
