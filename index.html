How a round works

  1. Set the bits A decimal on the right means you build it. Click a column to turn that bit on or off. The row clears when the 1s add up.
  2. Read the bits Bits already filled, and a blank on the right, means you read them. Type the decimal and press Enter.
  3. Stay ahead of the column Warm-up is slow. After that, pick 1, 2, or 5 minutes and score as many points as you can. A faster row is worth more. Six rows fit. The next arrival ends the round.

Place values: 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1. A 1 adds its place. A 0 adds nothing.
