How a row works

  1. Hex on the right. Tap bits until they match that digit (or byte). The line under the hex is your running sum.
  2. Bits already set. Type the hex on the keypad. One digit for a nibble, two for a byte.
  3. A miss stays up. Read the working, then try once more for half credit.
  4. Warm-up then race. First four clears are nibbles. Then full bytes. After eight clean clears, pick 1, 2, or 5 minutes.

Tap a row to select it. On a bit row, keys 1–4 or 1–8 flip places from the left. On a hex row, the keypad pops up. P pauses.

Ways to see a nibble

  • A is 10 — bits 1010 — places 8 + 2.
  • F is 15 — all four bits on. 0 is all off.
  • A byte is two nibbles: 4D is 0100 1101 — high nibble 4, low nibble D.
  • Same place values as Binary; hex is just grouping them by fours.
