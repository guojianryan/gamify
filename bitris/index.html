How a clear works

  1. Drop bits. Each filled cell adds its place value.
  2. Hit the target. The number on the right is the sum you need — exact only.
  3. Clear the row. Matching rows vanish and the stack drops.
  4. Overshoot? Bits wipe, same target — try that sum again.

Keys: ← → move, ↑ or X rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop, P pause.

Ways to see a byte

  • The rightmost column is worth 1. Odd targets always need that bit on.
  • Take the largest place that still fits the target, then solve what is left.
  • A clear shows the sum in place values — same arithmetic as the Binary drill, different motion.
